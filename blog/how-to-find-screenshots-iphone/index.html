How to Find Screenshots on iPhone—and Find the One You Need

By Michael Rode · September 21, 2026 · 4 min read · How-To

Quick answer: Open Photos, choose Collections, then find Media Types and tap Screenshots. To locate a particular image, try Photos Search with a distinctive word, date, or caption. If it is missing, check whether you saved it to Files, deleted it, or never saved the capture to your photo library.

You are at the counter, the return code is somewhere in your phone, and you have already scrolled past the same twenty photos twice. First narrow the library to screenshots. Then search using the detail you remember.

Where is the Screenshots collection on iPhone?

In the current Photos layout, open Collections → Media Types → Screenshots. Apple lists that route in its screenshot guide. If your iOS version has an Albums tab instead, look there for Media Types and Screenshots.

This collection brings screenshots together without requiring you to make an album manually. If you visit it often, look for the option to pin the collection. Apple’s media-type guide explains that shortcut.

How do you search for text inside a screenshot?

Open Search in Photos and try a distinctive piece of text, such as a business name or email address. Apple’s search guide lists text, dates, and captions among supported search terms.

Start with the most distinctive word you remember. “Receipt” could match dozens of images; the shop’s name is a better first attempt. If a long confirmation number fails, try a shorter word printed beside it. Text recognition can make mistakes, so one empty result is a reason to try a different clue.

Once you have opened the right screenshot, Live Text may let you copy the information inside it. Finding an image across your library and selecting text within an open image are separate steps. Apple describes the latter in its guide to interacting with image content.

What should you check if the screenshot is missing?

  1. Check the save destination. If you chose Save to Files, search Files rather than Photos. A full-page document may have been saved as a PDF.
  2. Check deletion. Look in Photos’ Recently Deleted collection if you recently cleaned up your library.
  3. Check what you actually captured. An image someone sent you may be present in Photos without appearing as a screenshot you took on this device.
  4. Check whether you saved it. Copying or sharing a capture and discarding it does not give you a saved photo to retrieve later.

Search by the approximate date as another route back. Think of the event around the capture: ordering dinner, booking a train, or choosing a gift. That context can narrow a visual search when the wording is gone.

How can you make important screenshots easier to find next time?

Add one piece of context when the image is still fresh. A caption such as “return code for blue jacket” gives you a phrase you are likely to remember. The photo-notes guide compares captions with longer notes and visible annotations.

Screenshot Swipe combines screenshot review with notes, tags, and on-device OCR search. Its value is having those organizing actions in the same workflow; Apple Photos also offers text and caption search. You can compare the features in the current app listing.

Use one or two stable categories for information you repeatedly need. “Travel” and a short destination note may be enough for bookings. When a saved image requires action, pair it with a scheduled reminder.

What else should you know?

Where are screenshots stored on an iPhone?

Screenshots saved to your photo library can be viewed in the Screenshots media-type collection in Photos. A capture saved to Files belongs in that app instead. Start by recalling the save option you chose, especially for a full-page webpage that you saved as a document.

Why can’t I find the Screenshots album?

The location depends on the Photos layout on your iOS version. Current Apple instructions use Collections, then Media Types, then Screenshots. Older layouts put Media Types under Albums. Look for the media-type group instead of assuming the collection must be among albums you created yourself.

Can I search screenshots by the words inside them?

Photos supports searching text in images. Try a distinctive word you remember seeing, then change the query if it returns nothing useful. A caption you added yourself provides another clue. An unsuccessful text search does not establish that the screenshot was deleted or never saved.

Can an organizer recover a screenshot I permanently deleted?

Do not install an organizer expecting it to reverse permanent deletion. First check Recently Deleted and any separate copies you know you made. Organizing apps are most useful for images they can access in your existing library, so focus on protecting and labeling the captures you still have.

What is the fastest search sequence to try?

  1. Open the Screenshots collection to narrow the pile.
  2. Search a distinctive word or date, then try a second clue.
  3. Check Files and Recently Deleted if needed; add context once you find the image.
