How to Delete Screenshots on iPhone Without Losing the Keepers

By Michael Rode · July 18, 2026 · Updated September 21, 2026 · 4 min read · How-To

Quick answer: Open Photos, go to Collections, then Media Types and Screenshots. Tap Select, choose the images you want to remove, and confirm deletion with the trash button. Review important keepers before using Select All. With iCloud Photos enabled, deletions affect the synced library; Recently Deleted provides a temporary recovery window.

You open Screenshots intending to remove ten expired delivery updates and discover hundreds of other captures. The fastest useful cleanup starts with a batch you understand. Keep your attention on what you can confidently remove.

Where do you select screenshots for deletion?

In the current Photos layout, use Collections → Media Types → Screenshots. If your version has an Albums tab, look there for Media Types. Apple’s screenshot instructions describe the current location.

Take a quick look through the batch before selecting everything. Return codes, booking details, and instructions can have a longer useful life than the other images around them. For keepers, add a short note or put them in an album you can revisit.

How do you delete multiple screenshots at once?

  1. Open the Screenshots collection and tap Select.
  2. Select the screenshots you have decided to remove. Use Select All only when you intend to remove the entire displayed collection, or carefully deselect every keeper.
  3. Tap the trash button and review the confirmation before deleting.

Apple documents bulk selection and deletion in Delete photos on your iPhone or iPad. A separate album is an organizational view of a library item, so filing a screenshot there does not protect it from a library deletion.

When you are uncertain, use several smaller batches. The extra confirmation gives you a chance to notice a record you still need. You can return to older captures after finishing the recent, obvious rejects.

What happens to deleted screenshots and iCloud copies?

Apple keeps deleted library items in Recently Deleted for 30 days. With iCloud Photos enabled, deletion also affects other devices using the same synced library. You can recover items during the recovery window; clearing them permanently removes that option. See Apple’s deletion and recovery guide.

Check the exact images before permanently clearing anything. A screenshot that matters to a warranty, trip, or work project deserves the same care as another photo you want to keep.

Why might storage still look full afterward?

Deleting a set of screenshots may not address the largest source of storage use. Check Settings → General → iPhone Storage to see what is occupying space. Your original library items and optimized local copies can also differ in size. Apple explains those storage options in its photo-storage guide.

Use your own before-and-after storage reading. The number of deleted images alone does not tell you how much local space was recovered. If the biggest category is a different app or a collection of large videos, continue investigating that category rather than repeatedly deleting useful screenshots.

What else should you know?

How do I delete all screenshots on iPhone?

Open the Screenshots collection, enter Select mode, and use Select All if it is available in your current view. Review the selection before confirming deletion. If you want to preserve some images, deselect them carefully or work in smaller batches so you can verify each decision.

Will deleting a screenshot also remove it from an album?

Albums organize images from your photo library. Removing the library item also affects where it appears in those albums. Use Remove from Album when your intention is only to change organization, and review a library deletion separately when your intention is to remove the image itself.

Can I recover a screenshot after deleting it?

Look in Recently Deleted immediately if you removed something by mistake. Apple provides a temporary recovery window for deleted library items. If you permanently cleared the item or that window has expired, look for a separate copy you previously saved rather than expecting a cleanup app to recover it.

Should I empty Recently Deleted after every cleanup?

You can leave the recovery window in place while you check that you kept everything important. Permanently clearing the collection is a separate decision. If you need storage urgently, inspect the items carefully first and confirm that you understand which images will no longer be available to recover.

How can you finish a careful cleanup session?

  1. Review the batch and identify every screenshot you still need.
  2. Delete selected rejects and verify that the keepers remain accessible.
  3. Check storage only if that was your goal, then schedule a small future review.
